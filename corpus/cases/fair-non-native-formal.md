# Evaluation of the caching layer

In addition, it should be noted that the caching layer was evaluated during the last
quarter. Moreover, the results are presented in this section.

As a result of the evaluation, we can say that the hit rate is 87 percent, which is
measured by the counters exported to the metrics backend and visible in the shared
dashboard.

Furthermore, the average latency was reduced from 240 milliseconds to 90 milliseconds after
the change, according to the same dashboard.

In conclusion, the caching layer works as it was expected, and we recommend to keep it in
the current configuration.
